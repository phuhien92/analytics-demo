States

Six states, one vocabulary. Hover steps a surface one rung down the surface ladder; active goes one further; selected is the only state that uses accent as a fill. Everything here is real — tab through it.

Action states

resthoveractivefocusdisabled primary secondary quiet

Disabled is never colour-only: it loses its fill contrast and carries aria-disabled, and any control that can be disabled must say why in adjacent text.

Focus ring

3px accent, 2px offset, outside the element's box, identical on every focusable thing including rows, disclosures, chart-table toggles and links. It is never removed, never recoloured, and never replaced by a border change.

Selection

Accent-soft fill plus the word "selected". The word is not decoration — it is the requirement.

Motion

State changes cross-fade colour over 110ms; anything that moves or resizes takes 180ms; overlays 280ms. Nothing animates position by more than a few pixels, and nothing loops.

Under prefers-reduced-motion: reduce every duration collapses to 1ms globally — states still change, they just arrive instantly.
