Colour

One accent carries every piece of emphasis. Semantic colour never carries meaning by itself: each of the three semantic grounds below is paired with a word and a mark, so removing colour loses nothing.

Accent

The single emphasis colour: focus rings, the primary action, the one thing on a screen that wants the eye. If two things on a screen are accent-coloured, one of them is wrong. Azure is bright enough that it splits in two — the fill colour is too light to also serve as text on a soft ground, so accent-coloured text uses the darker accent.ink.

rules

Colour is never the only carrier of meaning — every semantic ground ships with its label and mark. Accent is reserved; it is not a decoration. The word "verified" does not appear in product copy: say what was checked.
